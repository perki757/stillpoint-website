How it works

A practice, not an app

Stillpoint creates a moment of calm in your day. At your chosen time, your phone gently pauses and invites you to reflect.

1

Pause

At your chosen daily time, Stillpoint gently shields your other apps, creating space for reflection.

2

Reflect

Note what you're grateful for and what you've achieved. One screen, one thought at a time.

3

Receive

An AI-crafted affirmation built from your words, your patterns, and your growth over time.

4

Return

Your phone unlocks. Three minutes of presence, and you carry that stillness into your day.

Privacy

Your thoughts stay yours

Stillpoint is built local-first. Your journal entries are stored on your device, encrypted by iOS Data Protection. We don't have accounts, we don't run ads, and we don't sell data.

Local-first storage

All entries are stored on-device with iOS encryption. No cloud sync, no server-side storage of personal reflections.

No account required

Start immediately. No email, no sign-up, no tracking. Your practice is between you and your phone.

No ads, ever

Stillpoint is supported by an optional premium subscription. Your attention is never the product.

Self-control, not surveillance

The screen lock uses Apple's Screen Time API with individual authorisation. You opt in, you control it, you can disable it anytime.
