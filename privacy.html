Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Stillpoint is built with privacy at its core. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Summary

Your journal entries are stored only on your device. Some of what you write is sent to our AI provider each time a reflection is written, as described below. We don't have user accounts. We don't run ads. We don't sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

The following data is created and stored only on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection encryption:

  • Your entries for both daily questions (what you are grateful for, and what you did for yourself)
  • Your reflections
  • Emotion check-in responses: the feeling you choose, your energy level, and your answer to the view question ("how much of the view is this one?")
  • Your Stillpoint paragraph and its fragments
  • Notes you set down on heavier days
  • Self-discovery prompt responses
  • Onboarding preferences (name, tone, reflection time, chosen companion)
  • Session history and streak data
  • Your app settings

We keep no copy of this data unless you choose to send us feedback (see below). Some of it is sent each time a reflection is written, as described under "Data sent to our servers". You can export or delete all of it at any time from the app's Settings.

Self-discovery prompt responses are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers and are never used to train any AI model. If you delete the app or your device, these responses are gone with it.

Data sent to our servers

During your free trial (your first seven sessions), and whenever you have a subscription or a bursary, the app sends the following to our server each time it writes your reflection. Our server passes it to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the reflection:

  • Your first name
  • Today's entries for both questions, and the feeling and energy level you chose
  • Your entries and feelings from up to seven of your earlier sessions, with each entry shortened
  • Your Stillpoint paragraph and its fragments, once you have written them
  • On some days, depending on how you answer the check-in, up to three notes you set down on earlier days
  • Your onboarding answers (such as tone, values and coping style), your companion, your streak, and how many sessions you have completed
  • A device identifier: a random ID that Apple gives this app on your iPhone. It is used only to limit how many reflections one device can request each day.

Your self-discovery answers are not sent. Your answer to the view question is not sent either: the app uses it on your iPhone only to decide whether to include set-down notes.

This data is:

  • Transmitted over HTTPS encryption
  • Not stored by our server. Our server passes it to Anthropic and returns the reflection to the app.
  • Processed by Anthropic only to generate your reflection. Anthropic does not use it to train its AI models. Anthropic automatically deletes it within 30 days, unless it is flagged under Anthropic's Usage Policy (then kept for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to be kept longer.

What our server does keep: to prevent abuse, it keeps a daily count of reflection requests for each device and for each IP address. These records are stored under one-way hashes of your device identifier and your IP address, not the identifier or address itself, and contain nothing you wrote. Our server logs also record the hashed IP address with each reflection request. The daily counts are deleted automatically within a few days, and server logs after 30 days.

If you are past your free trial and have no subscription or bursary, you receive pre-written reflections. For these, the app makes no reflection request: nothing from your session is sent to our server or to Anthropic to write them.

Reflection feedback (optional)

If you long-press a past reflection and tell us how it landed, we send a small piece of feedback to our Firebase server so we can improve the AI reflection prompt. The feedback record we store contains:

  • Your rating ("didn't land", "landed", or "really landed")
  • Your optional written comment, which only the "didn't land" flow asks for
  • The prompt version that generated the reflection, the app version, the reflection's date and an ID for it, and whether it was written by the AI or pre-written
  • Your device identifier (the random ID described above, not linked to your name or email)
  • The reflection text itself, only if you leave the "Include this reflection and what you wrote today" switch on. This works the same way for every rating.

With the switch on, the app also sends what you wrote in that session: your entries for both questions, and the feeling and energy you chose. Our server does not store this part or pass it on.

If you choose "didn't land", we also receive an email with your comment, the reflection text (if the switch was on), and the versions, date and IDs listed above. This email is sent through Resend, our email provider.

Whichever rating you choose, the switch is shown before anything is sent, and turning it off sends your rating and comment without the reflection or your entries. We use this data only to improve the AI reflection prompt. It is not used to train AI models. We keep feedback until you ask us to delete it.

Screen Time API

Stillpoint uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls framework) with individual authorisation. This means:

  • You choose to opt in during onboarding or in Settings
  • The app can temporarily shield other apps at your chosen daily time
  • No information about your app usage, screen time, or browsing is collected or transmitted
  • You can disable the screen lock at any time in Settings or via the emergency override
  • You can revoke authorisation entirely via iOS Settings > Screen Time

Location

Stillpoint does not request or use your location. The greeting changes with the time on your iPhone's clock.

Analytics

Stillpoint uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to identify crashes. This data is not linked to your name, email, or any account, because the app has no accounts. It includes:

  • App launches, session completions, and feature usage
  • The feeling you select from the wheel and the energy level you place, recorded as the words and the number themselves
  • Your answer to the view question, recorded as a position on a scale from 0 to 10
  • The companion, element and tone you chose during onboarding
  • How many entries you write, and how long some answers are, as a count or a rough size, never the words
  • Which parts of the app you open, and when, including the time of day
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Device type and OS version

What we want to be straightforward about: these events are recorded against a pseudonymous identifier: a random ID that is stable for as long as the app stays installed on your device. That means the record is not only a set of totals. It can show one device's sequence of events over time, including the feelings, energy levels and view answers chosen on different days. We use this to understand whether the app actually helps people over weeks rather than days, which is difficult to learn any other way.

What this record does not contain: anything you write. Your gratitude entries, what you did for yourself, your self-discovery answers, your Stillpoint paragraph and fragments, your set-down notes and your reflections are never included in analytics. The identifier is not linked to your name or email, we do not attach any user ID of our own, we do not attempt to work out who a device belongs to, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA) are collected.

This analytics data is retained for a maximum of 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Bursary

Stillpoint offers a bursary for users who cannot afford a subscription. When you apply for a bursary, we collect:

  • Your email address (for bursary confirmation)
  • A short reflection on why a daily practice matters to you
  • Your intended usage frequency
  • Optional marketing consent

This data is stored on our Firebase server. It is emailed to us, and a confirmation is emailed to you, through Resend, our email provider. Your email address is also recorded in our server logs when you apply. Your email will only be used for bursary-related communication (and marketing updates if you opt in). The bursary grants 60 days of full premium access. The access expiry date is also stored locally on your device. We keep your application until you ask us to delete it.

Android waitlist

If you join the Android waitlist on our website, your email address and your optional answer to the app-blocking question are held by Kit (kit.com), our email list provider. We use them only to email you about Stillpoint on Android. To be removed, contact us at hello@stillpoint.tools.

Subscriptions

Stillpoint offers three monthly subscription tiers, all handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store:

  • Support: $12.99/month
  • Standard: $7.99/month
  • Community: $3.99/month

All three tiers provide identical premium features. The different price points exist so you can choose what feels right for you. We do not collect or store your payment information. Subscription management and billing are governed by Apple's terms.

Children's privacy

Stillpoint is suitable for users aged 13 and over. The app stores data locally on the user's device and does not require an account. Parents and guardians can review or delete a user's data at any time using the Export and Delete features in Settings.

Data export and deletion

You can export all your data as a JSON file or permanently delete all data from within the app's Settings screen. Since data is stored locally, deleting the app also removes all data stored on your device. It does not remove reflection feedback or a bursary application you have sent us. To have those deleted, contact us.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights directly using the Export and Delete features in the app's Settings. For data we hold on our server, such as reflection feedback or a bursary application, contact us.

If you have any questions or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@stillpoint.tools. We will respond within 30 days.

Data controller

The data controller for Stillpoint is Will Perkins, operating as a sole trader based in England. For data protection enquiries, contact hello@stillpoint.tools.

Third-party data processors

These companies process personal data on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud (Firebase Cloud Functions, Firestore and Cloud Logging): runs our server. It passes reflection requests between the app and Anthropic, and holds the rate-limit records and server logs, reflection feedback and bursary applications described above.
  • Anthropic: provides the Claude AI model that writes reflections, and receives the data listed under "Data sent to our servers". Anthropic processes this data only to generate your reflection and does not use it to train its AI models. Anthropic automatically deletes it within 30 days, unless it is flagged under Anthropic's Usage Policy (then kept for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to be kept longer. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Google (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics): collects usage and crash data against a pseudonymous per-install identifier, as described under Analytics above. Does not receive your journal entries. See Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Resend: sends our emails: bursary applications and confirmations, and "didn't land" reflection feedback.
  • Kit: holds the Android waitlist, if you join it on our website.
  • Cloudflare: routes email sent to hello@stillpoint.tools on to our mailbox.
  • Our email mailbox provider: holds the emails you send us and our copies of bursary and feedback emails.

Some of these companies process data outside the UK, including in the United States. No other third parties receive your personal data.
